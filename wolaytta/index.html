Privacy Policy — Wolaytta Dictionary

Effective: August 8, 2026  ·  Operated by Adiel Labs  ·  bogalehabtama@gmail.com

Data We Collect

Wolaytta Dictionary does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no servers.

  • Search queries are processed entirely on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • Favorites are saved in a local database on your device only. Adiel Labs has no access to them.
  • The dictionary itself ships inside the app, so lookups work with no internet connection.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.

Adiel Labs does not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Uninstalling the app deletes its local database, including your favorites.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays a banner ad served by Google AdMob. This is the only feature that uses the internet.

Wolaytta Dictionary requests non-personalized ads only. The app is tagged as child-directed in its ad configuration, which instructs Google not to use advertising identifiers to build a profile or personalize ads for you. Ads are selected from the general context, not from your history.

Ad content is also capped at a G rating — suitable for general audiences, including families — because a dictionary is open to readers of any age.

To serve and measure those ads, Google may still process:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction events, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by Adiel Labs. We never receive it. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in non-personalized ads for details.

On iOS, the system may ask for your permission to track before ads load. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not change what the app does — ads remain non-personalized either way.

On Android, you can further limit ad tracking via Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. As described above, it requests non-personalized ads so that no advertising profile is built from its use. If you believe your child has been affected in any way, contact us at bogalehabtama@gmail.com.

A dictionary is useful to readers of any age, so the app is designed to meet the requirements that apply to children's audiences: the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the child-data provisions of the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and the Google Play Families policy. No behavioral advertising is served to any user of this app.

Permissions

Wolaytta Dictionary requests internet access, which is used only to load the banner ad. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise location.
